Privacy statement

Last updated: September 22, 2026

InjOffice is provided by Injecting Inc. This statement covers injoffice.com, our public browser playground, and official InjOffice desktop builds. For privacy questions or requests, email nick.pismenkov@injecting.ai.

Your documents

The desktop editor processes documents on your computer. The public browser playground processes files you open in your browser. These editing features do not upload your document contents to Injecting Inc. or an AI provider, and do not require an account. The public playground’s agent demonstration uses simulated responses, not a remote AI service.

The desktop app stores preferences, recent-file information (including file names and paths), and recovery copies locally. Files you save remain where you choose to save them. Your operating system, backup software, or a folder-sync service you use may separately copy those files.

This statement does not describe third-party integrations or separately operated, self-hosted deployments. If you use a configured server, collaboration service, or AI provider in another deployment, its operator’s privacy terms apply to information sent there.

Website visits, downloads, and updates

Amazon Web Services hosts and delivers injoffice.com. To deliver a page, the hosting service receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time.

The download page contacts GitHub to find the latest desktop release. Installer downloads and desktop update checks also contact GitHub and its download infrastructure. Those services receive connection information such as your IP address and request headers; a download request also identifies the requested release and platform. These requests do not include your document contents.

You can turn off automatic desktop update checks in App updates. Manually checking for or downloading an update still makes a network request. Visiting our source repository or filing an issue also uses GitHub.

These providers handle information under their own policies: AWS Privacy Notice and GitHub Privacy Statement. Their infrastructure may process information in the United States and other countries.

Cookies, preferences, and analytics

Our website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. It uses your browser’s local storage to remember your light or dark theme. You can remove this preference by clearing site data in your browser. Fonts are served with the website, rather than requested from Google Fonts.

The official desktop app does not include automatic usage analytics or automatic crash-report uploads. We do not sell document contents or use them for advertising or AI model training.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever message, diagnostics, or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond, investigate problems, and manage your request. Our email service processes those messages to deliver and store them. Please avoid sending sensitive documents unless needed for your request.

GitHub issues and comments are public. Do not post private documents or personal information there. Use the email above for privacy requests.

Retention and your choices

We do not hold a server-side copy of documents you only edit locally. Local preferences, recent-file records, and recovery data can remain on your device until removed; uninstalling the app may leave application data behind. Contact us if you need help removing local data, and save any documents you want to keep first.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Provider-managed records follow the providers’ retention practices. You can ask us to delete support information we hold about you.

Where applicable law gives you these rights, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of personal information we hold, and object to its processing. Email nick.pismenkov@injecting.ai. We may need to verify your request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We cannot access or delete files stored only on your device.

Where a legal basis is required, we process service and support information for our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and supporting InjOffice, to fulfill a service you request, or to meet legal obligations, as applicable.

Changes to this statement

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be highlighted here or through another appropriate notice.
