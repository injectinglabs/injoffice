InjOffice vs LibreOffice

LibreOffice is a mature, complete office suite. InjOffice is a young editor built around one different idea: change the original Office file in place and keep everything else as it was. Here is where each one fits.

Both are free and open source. Updated 2026-09-23.

Where LibreOffice is better

  • Feature breadth. Word processing, spreadsheets, presentations, drawing, formulas and databases, each with years of features that InjOffice does not have: styles management, mail merge, pivot tables, advanced charts, slide design tools and much more.
  • Maturity. More than a decade of releases, a large user base and extensive documentation and community help.
  • Formats. It opens and saves OpenDocument, legacy Microsoft Office formats and many others. InjOffice opens only DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and PDF.
  • Layout and printing. It lays out and prints pages itself. InjOffice's document view flows on screen and does not reproduce Word's pagination.
  • Macros and extensions. It can automate work with macros and extend itself with add-ons. InjOffice runs neither.
  • Structural editing. Inserting rows and columns, reorganizing documents and building presentations from scratch are routine in LibreOffice and limited in InjOffice today.

If you need a full office suite, LibreOffice is the better choice today.

Where InjOffice is different

  • It keeps the file you were sent. Editing a DOCX, XLSX or PPTX changes the parts you touched and leaves the rest of the package as it was, so parts InjOffice does not understand are not dropped or rewritten on save.
  • It says what it cannot do. Commands it cannot perform safely on a particular file are disabled with a reason, and files it cannot handle, such as password-protected ones, are refused rather than opened in a way that could change them.
  • It is built for AI agents too. The same engines are available as libraries in which an agent proposes a change set, your application reviews and approves it, and the written file is verified. See InjOffice for AI agents.
  • It is small and local. No account, no upload, one editor for four formats.

Which to choose

Choose LibreOffice when you need a complete suite, work in OpenDocument or older formats, rely on macros, or need precise page layout. Choose InjOffice when you want to change an Office file you received and send it back with everything you did not touch intact, or when you are building software, including AI agents, that edits Office files. Many people will use both.
